Apply this rubric before deciding `passed`:

- Does the image's subject match what the brief's `prompt` asked for, not just loosely but in the specific composition described?
- Is the image free of obvious AI-generation artifacts: distorted hands or faces, garbled or nonsensical text rendered into the image, physically impossible objects?
- Is the image appropriate for a professional business audience — no content that would look out of place on a company blog?
- Does the image's aspect ratio and framing work for its intended placement (hero images need to work as a wide social-share image; inline images just need to make sense next to the surrounding paragraph)?

Fail the image if it violates any of these; do not pass an image out of leniency because regeneration is inconvenient.
