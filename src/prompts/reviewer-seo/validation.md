Apply this rubric before deciding `passed`:

- Does the focus keyword appear naturally at least once in the body, and ideally in the first paragraph, without being stuffed unnaturally into every section?
- Do the H2 headings read naturally to a human while still reflecting the topic's real search intent (never sacrifice readability purely to cram in a keyword)?
- Does the drafted `seoTitleDraft` still accurately describe the article as actually written, and does it stay near 60 characters?
- Does the drafted `metaDescriptionDraft` still accurately describe the article and stay in the 120-160 character range?
- Are any of the planned `seoKeywords` completely absent from the final draft in a way that suggests the article drifted from its planned topic?

Raise an issue only for a real, specific SEO defect — not a stylistic nitpick unrelated to search performance.
